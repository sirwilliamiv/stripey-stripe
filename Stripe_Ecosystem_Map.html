The shape of the stack

A map of how Stripe's products fit together — which ones are foundational rails, which consume others, which are developer tools that enable building, and which are suites that abstract many products behind a single integration. Products appear wherever they play a role, so several show up more than once.

Protocols & primitives beneath the agentic layer

Not standalone products — open or co-authored standards, payment tokens, and capabilities of products above. They're what the agentic suites are built from.

Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP) Machine Payments Protocol (MPP) Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) Shared Payment Tokens (SPTs) Link agent wallet Issuing for agents

This is a communication model, not Stripe's internal architecture. "Consumes / built on / bundles" describe how products typically relate from a builder's point of view; exact dependencies vary by integration, and Stripe ships continuously. A product appears in more than one place when it plays more than one role — e.g. Radar is both a product and a cross-cutting enabler; Checkout is both a surface and an abstraction over Payments.
