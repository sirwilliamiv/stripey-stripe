# Stripe Product Catalog

*Every Stripe financial product — the problem it solves and where to learn more.*

**Compiled June 21, 2026** · 29 products across Stripe’s main product areas.

Descriptions summarize the core problem each product solves. Links point to Stripe’s official product or documentation pages. Stripe ships features continuously, so this reflects the lineup as of the compile date.

-----

## Payments

**Payments** — Lets businesses accept online card and local payments from customers worldwide without building their own payment-processing and PCI-compliance infrastructure.
<https://stripe.com/payments>

**Managed Payments** — Acts as merchant of record so businesses can sell globally while Stripe handles tax calculation, remittance, and compliance liability on their behalf.
<https://docs.stripe.com/payments/managed-payments>

**Terminal** — Brings Stripe’s payment stack into the physical world with card readers and SDKs, unifying in-person and online sales under one system.
<https://stripe.com/terminal>

**Connect** — Handles complex multi-party money flows for platforms and marketplaces, so they can onboard sellers, split payments, and pay out at scale.
<https://stripe.com/connect>

**Radar** — Uses machine learning trained on Stripe’s global network to detect and block fraudulent transactions and reduce chargebacks.
<https://stripe.com/radar>

**Link** — Stores and autofills a customer’s saved payment details across sites for faster, higher-converting one-click checkout.
<https://stripe.com/payments/link>

**Authorization Boost** — Optimizes acceptance rates by automatically updating cards, routing across networks, and intelligently retrying failed payments to recover lost revenue.
<https://docs.stripe.com/payments>

**Global Payouts** — Sends money to recipients, sellers, and contractors in 100+ countries via fiat or stablecoin from a single integration.
<https://docs.stripe.com/payouts>

**Identity** — Verifies the real-world identity of users by checking government IDs and selfies, streamlining KYC and reducing onboarding fraud.
<https://stripe.com/identity>

**Financial Connections** — Lets users securely link their bank accounts so businesses can verify account ownership, balances, and transaction data.
<https://stripe.com/financial-connections>

**Crypto** — Provides infrastructure to accept, on-ramp, pay out, and build with crypto and stablecoins alongside traditional money.
<https://stripe.com/crypto>

**Crypto Onramp** — An embeddable widget that lets a business’s users buy crypto with card or bank inside the business’s own app or site.
<https://docs.stripe.com/crypto/onramp>

**Climate** — Lets businesses direct a fraction of revenue toward vetted carbon-removal projects to fund permanent CO2 removal.
<https://stripe.com/climate>

## Revenue

**Billing** — Manages subscriptions and recurring revenue, handling plans, proration, trials, dunning, and failed-payment recovery.
<https://stripe.com/billing>

**Usage-based billing** — Meters consumption and prices on usage (seats, tokens, API calls) so businesses can monetize flexible and AI-style pricing models.
<https://stripe.com/billing/usage-based-billing>

**Invoicing** — Creates, customizes, and sends one-off or recurring invoices with automated accounts-receivable workflows and no code required.
<https://stripe.com/invoicing>

**Tax** — Automatically calculates, collects, and files sales tax, VAT, and GST across 100+ countries, monitoring registration thresholds.
<https://stripe.com/tax>

**Revenue Recognition** — Automates accrual accounting and turns raw transaction data into auditable, compliant revenue schedules and reports.
<https://stripe.com/revenue-recognition>

**Sigma** — An interactive SQL and AI environment for querying Stripe data directly to build custom reports without exporting it elsewhere.
<https://stripe.com/sigma>

**Data Pipeline** — Syncs a business’s complete Stripe data into its own data warehouse (Snowflake, Redshift) on a schedule, with no ETL to maintain.
<https://stripe.com/data-pipeline>

**Atlas** — Incorporates a startup end to end: company formation, EIN, bank account, equity, and 83(b) filing in a few days.
<https://stripe.com/atlas>

## Money management

**Issuing** — Lets businesses programmatically create and control physical and virtual payment cards for spend, expenses, or their own customers.
<https://stripe.com/issuing>

**Treasury** — Banking-as-a-service APIs that let platforms embed financial accounts so their users can store, send, and receive money.
<https://stripe.com/treasury>

**Capital** — Offers fast, data-driven financing and loans to businesses (and a platform’s users) based on their Stripe payment history.
<https://stripe.com/capital>

## Prebuilt checkout components

**Checkout** — A prebuilt, Stripe-hosted payment page that supports many payment methods and optimizations with minimal integration work.
<https://stripe.com/payments/checkout>

**Payment Links** — Generates shareable no-code payment URLs so businesses can sell or collect payment without building any website.
<https://stripe.com/payments/payment-links>

**Elements** — Customizable, drop-in UI components for embedding a fully branded payment form directly into a business’s own checkout.
<https://stripe.com/payments/elements>

## Platform extensions (for Connect platforms)

**Treasury for platforms** — Lets software platforms offer embedded banking and financial accounts to their connected users out of the box.
<https://stripe.com/treasury>

**Capital for platforms** — Lets platforms offer their own connected sellers and merchants access to financing, expanding revenue and retention.
<https://stripe.com/capital/platforms>
